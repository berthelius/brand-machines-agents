# Brand Machines editorial identity

Operational translation of the editorial instructions authorized by Viktor Berthelius: AGENTS.md, the Spanish glossary and style guide, DESIGN.md, and the English edition's canon and glossary. Consulted on 27 September 2026; source snapshots are identified in references/provenance.json at skill level.

The voice is philosophical, systematic, technical and editorial. Prose uses precise terms and verifiable data. It does not use emojis or capitals for emphasis. Avoid unsupported claims such as “market leader” and generic promotional language such as “best-in-class.” Mark unverifiable data as pending rather than fabricating a correction.

The canon distinguishes emergent coherence from uniformity. The definition of Brand Machine and its seven layers preserve their meaning across languages and formats. The Core consists of purpose, actionable values and vision.

The editorial design system uses EB Garamond, Source Sans 3 and Source Code Pro. Expression is monochrome by default. The #e63946 accent is reserved for authorized media and uses; the printed interior is monochrome. This identity belongs to Brand Machines. It is not a compulsory visual template for other brands.

Governance protects the canon and records decisions and versions. Production tools serve the method. An exported format or a coherence approval does not authorize publication, canon changes or intervention in another brand.
