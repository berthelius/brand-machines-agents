# Brand validation — Chapter 17, Section 17.2

The English book presents a request with type copy, context email_subject and content “The BEST offer of the year!!!!”.

Its illustrative response identifies no_superlativos and exclamaciones_excesivas and suggests “An offer you won’t want to miss”. The example demonstrates request, comparison, diagnosis and suggestion. It does not demonstrate improved conversion or impose a universal prohibition on exclamation marks. The rule IDs remain unchanged in both book languages.

In this pack, the checks apply only to email_subject and marketing_copy. The threshold of two ¡ or ! signs is an implementation choice for this demonstration, not a number prescribed by the book. Literal matches require contextual review. The English terms “the best”, “market leader” and “best-in-class” implement the translated example and editorial vocabulary.
